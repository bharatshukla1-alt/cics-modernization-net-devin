## Move group summary and rationale

Grouping rule (create-tran-group agent): same mapset, or linked by XCTL/PF-key branching. CC00/CM00/CA00 are the hub every screen returns to; linking through them would put the whole estate in one group, so the hub is its own group (MG-02), and functional groups are formed from the direct links *between* their programs plus their shared return menu.
